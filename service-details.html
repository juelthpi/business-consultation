Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the setup process take?

The typical business setup process takes 3-5 business days for free zone companies and 5-7 days for mainland companies, depending on the business activity and documentation readiness.

Do I need to be present in Dubai?

While physical presence is required for certain steps like visa stamping and Emirates ID registration, we can handle most of the process remotely. We'll guide you on when your presence is necessary.

What documents do I need?

Basic requirements include passport copies, visa copies (if applicable), business plan, and proof of address. Specific requirements vary based on business activity and structure. We'll provide a complete checklist during consultation.

Can I change my business activity later?

Yes, you can add or modify business activities during license renewal or through an amendment process. We can assist with activity amendments at any time.
